Data Model & Storage Design

SQLite database tables (JOB, USER) and file-system entities (GAME_CONFIG, TRAINING_CONFIG, RUN, CHECKPOINT)

JOB and USER are real SQLite tables (control_center.db). GAME_CONFIG, TRAINING_CONFIG, RUN, and CHECKPOINT are file-system entities (JSON files / directories / .pth files) — not DB tables.
